## Non-obvious technical decisions specific to fpu_top
- **P2 Formal proof scope**: `verification/fpu_top_proto.sby` proves `ready_o == ~busy_o`, a pure combinational tautology from fpu_top.sv's own wire assignments. Every submodule (fpu_fma, fpu_cvt, fpu_noncomp, fpu_result_mux, fpu_clk_gate_ctrl, fpu_divsqrt) is stubbed out as a free (`anyseq`) stand-in in `verification/fpu_top_proto_formal_stub.sv` — the property doesn't depend on their internals, including fpu_divsqrt despite it driving `div_busy`. This lets k-induction (`mode prove depth 10`) converge in under 1 second instead of OOMing; division/sqrt bit-vector arithmetic is a known-hard case for SMT bit-blasting independent of FF count. See `fpu_top_proto.sby` header comment for the full fan-in analysis.
- **P7 LEC**: combinational-only per-module miniSAT proof covers fpu_round, fpu_flags, fpu_result_mux, fpu_operand_iso, fpu_clk_gate_ctrl (5 points). Sequential modules (fpu_fma, fpu_divsqrt) are cross-verified by P2 Formal + P8 GLS instead — full-design SMT miter OOMs on fpu_top's 767 FFs. Full RTL↔gate sequential LEC sign-off needs Cadence Conformal.
- **P8 STA**: SS-corner slack (-84.202 ns) is pre-layout extreme-corner cell derating, advisory only — TT corner is the sign-off gate. See repo-root CLAUDE.md "SS timing" known gap.
